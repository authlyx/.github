## Features

- **Sign-in your way.** Username and password, license key, or device login with no form at all.
- **Device locks.** Tie each license to a PC and set how many devices an account can use.
- **Plans and features.** Give each plan its own features and metadata, and read them from your app.
- **Variables and scripts.** Change settings remotely and ship signed scripts without a new build.
- **Offline access.** Customers keep working without internet, using a signed permit saved on their PC.
- **Updates.** Set the current version, allowed versions and download links. The SDKs show the update message for you.
- **Sessions and logs.** See who is signed in, end sessions, and read your app's logs.
- **Signed responses.** Every server response is signed, and the SDKs reject anything tampered with.

## Bots

Manage your apps from Discord or Telegram: look up users, create licenses, reset devices and more. Add the hosted bot, or run your own copy with a Bot Key from the dashboard.

---

## Getting started

1. Create an account and an app in the [dashboard](https://dash.authlyx.dev).
2. Copy your app key from the app's credentials.
3. Follow [Connect your app](https://docs.authlyx.dev/guides/quickstart) for your language.
